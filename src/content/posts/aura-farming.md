# Farming Aura

Maybe emotional intelligence is a path to "farming aura." An interesting ability in today's world is to read the environment and think: how can I bring the word of the gospel into this setting?

Unless you are inside a church or an environment with shared faith, it is wise to have a non-aggressive communication style and preach the gospel in the way that works, combining non-aggressive communication with humor can be a path.

It is interesting to learn from the gospel and bring the word into everyday life. From time to time, letting out a phrase like "I sinned" or "confess the little cow" might be a path. I really like learning by doing, and this is something I am doing at the moment.

As Saint Philip Neri taught, humor is a path to holiness. I try to use humor and non-aggressive communication to slowly plant small seeds.

Young people today often use the phrase "farming aura," and I see that the greatest way to farm aura is by going out and preaching the gospel. If necessary, use words like Saint Francis of Assisi would.

As you mature, you begin to see that it is not possible to be two people and worship many things at the same time. Christ is the end, the beginning, and the middle, as Raul Seixas would say.

Even though the singer was referring to Hinduism, I see that the example can also be used in Catholicism.

As you received freely, give freely. One thing that helps me a lot in my work is trying to be good, to be a good person, to understand every kind of person, to try to put myself in the place of everyone, to understand that person's environment and their vision, discussing rarely is the path; if you do not use non-aggressive communication in a discussion, it can lead to infinite friction.

I have a lot to learn, but one thing that makes a big difference in daily life is being good to people, listening, paying attention, and noticing each person who comes to speak with you or who is not doing well. Everyone has a soul and was created in the image and likeness of God.

If you want to make life easier, be good, serve, or rather, be excellent at serving and think: how can I make the path easier for those who seek me? We help each other and those who seek us, but do not do it expecting anything in return. It may not come, and that's okay.

The greatest way to farm aura is by being an imitation of Christ, the way, the truth, and the life.

Pax et Bonum
